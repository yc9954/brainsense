14 months ago, I launched my meeting-notes app from my kitchen table.

Today, we just passed 1,000 paying users.

I'm a solo founder, so every bug, support ticket, and win has my name on it.

The lesson I learned the hard way? I spent 6 months building features nobody asked for.

Growth only started when I stopped guessing and sat in on 50+ customer calls.

People didn't want a smarter app. They wanted 10 minutes back after every meeting.

Build less. Listen more.

Thank you to the first 1,000 who trusted me. On to the next 10k.
